Refund Policy

Effective: August 2026

1. Job Post Payments

  • Full Refund: Full refund within 24 hours of payment if the job post has not yet gone live.
  • No Refund: No refund after the job post has been activated and gone live in candidate matching.
  • Technical Failures: 100% full refund guaranteed if technical issues prevent your post from going live or matching candidates.
  • Policy Violations: No refund if a job post is removed for violating our Terms of Service.

2. Candidate Membership

  • Non-Refundable: The membership fee (₹99/year in India, $9/year globally) grants access to the Wrkmark Jobs platform for one year. As the service begins immediately upon activation (your profile goes live in matching), the subscription fee is non-refundable once your profile has been activated.
  • Technical Failure Exception: If a technical failure prevents your candidate profile from going live within 48 hours of payment, a 100% full refund is guaranteed.

3. Refund Process

To request a refund under the eligible conditions above, please send an email to billing@wrkmark.com with the following details:

Subject: Refund Request — [Job Post Title / Candidate Account]
Details: Account Email, Payment Date / Transaction ID
  • We will acknowledge and review your request within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer or banking provider.
